## 📝 Executive Summary
This project addresses a critical business need: **identifying the drivers of profitability and sales growth in a competitive retail environment.** Using a dataset of 2022 transactions, I developed an interactive Tableau dashboard that provides executive-level KPIs and granular subcategory analysis. 

The dashboard empowers stakeholders to move beyond "what happened" to "why it happened," specifically focusing on the 29.5% YoY sales growth and the efficiency of product-level profit margins.

---

## 🛠️ Business Problems Solved
1. **Identifying Value Drivers:** Determined which products contribute the most to the bottom line ($82K total profit) versus those that are simply high-volume but low-margin.
2. **Growth Tracking:** Quantified the exact percentage growth in Sales (+29.5%), Profit (+32.7%), and Quantity (+23.3%) compared to the previous year (PY) to validate marketing and operational strategies.
3. **Inventory & Seasonality Planning:** Analyzed monthly trends to pinpoint the "December Peak," allowing for better supply chain and stock management in future cycles.
4. **Underperforming Segments:** Isolated subcategories (like Tables or Bookcases) that show potential "Profit Leaks" despite decent sales volume.

---

## 🔍 In-Depth Data Analysis & Insights

### 1. The "December Surge" vs. Q1 Slump
* **Insight:** There is a significant spike in both Sales and Profit in **December**, reaching the highest month of the year. Conversely, **January** starts at a yearly low.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** The business should implement "Post-Holiday" loyalty campaigns in January to smooth out the revenue dip and maintain momentum.

### 2. Profit-to-Sales Correlation
* **Insight:** While **Total Sales** grew by 29.5%, **Total Profit** outpaced it at 32.7%. 
* **Analysis:** This indicates that the company is not just selling more, but is selling more *efficiently*—likely through better pricing strategies or a shift toward higher-margin items like **Copiers** and **Accessories**.

### 3. Subcategory Profitability Heatmap
* **Insight:** **Chairs** and **Phones** are the primary revenue engines. However, the analysis reveals that **Tables** and **Bookcases** (marked with red indicators) are often sold at a loss or extremely thin margins.
* **Actionable Recommendation:** Review the discount structures for "Tables." If these are "loss leaders" meant to drive other sales, the strategy is working; if not, a price adjustment or vendor renegotiation is required.

### 4. Quantity vs. Value Discrepancy
* **Insight:** **Total Quantity** increased by 23.3%, which is lower than the Sales growth (29.5%).
* **Analysis:** This suggests an increase in **Average Order Value (AOV)**. Customers are buying more expensive items per transaction rather than just buying more items in bulk.

## 🚀 Technical Implementation
* **Data Cleaning:** Performed initial data auditing in Excel to handle null values and ensure date consistency for 2021-2022 comparisons.
* **Calculated Fields:** Developed custom Tableau calculations for **YoY Growth %** and **Profit Margin** to provide dynamic context to static numbers.
* **Visual Hierarchy:** Designed a "Top-Down" flow. Users start with high-level KPIs, move to trend analysis, and finish with granular subcategory comparisons.
* **Dual-Axis Charts:** Used dual-axis trend lines to compare current year performance against the previous year (shadow line) for instant context.
